summary: "A privacy-first job description analyzer that pulls out requirements, skills, salary and red flags, entirely in your browser."
problem: "Job descriptions are long, vague and sometimes contradictory. It is hard to tell what is truly required, what is nice-to-have, and which postings have red flags."
features:
  - "Extracts title, experience, education, skills, location, work mode, employment type and salary"
  - "Separates must-have from nice-to-have requirements"
  - "Flags contradictions, unrealistic experience claims and vague language"
  - "Optional local candidate-match score"
  - "Transparent: every conclusion links back to the matched text"
  - "No backend, no signup, no AI, and nothing is uploaded"
howItWorks: "The text is segmented into sections with heading heuristics, then field extractors match keywords and synonyms from curated dictionaries. Red flags come from deterministic pattern matching, not AI."
