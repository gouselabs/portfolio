## Features

- **New and pre-loved in one catalog.** Every listing shows its condition
  (e.g. *Like new*, *Pre-loved*) and its discount off the list price.
- **Search and browse.** Search by title, author or ISBN, plus a category bar
  and sidebar (Fiction, Self-Help, Business & Money, Technology and more) with
  live listing counts.
- **Book detail pages.** Price and savings breakdown, estimated delivery date,
  seller info, stock status, and a per-order quantity limit.
- **Cart, wishlist and checkout.** Add to cart, buy now, or save for later,
  with free delivery above a threshold and cash on delivery.
- **Sell your books.** Signed-in users can list books from their own shelf and
  show up as the seller on the listing.
